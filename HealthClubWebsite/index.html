WHO WE ARE

Our purpose. Our community.
Our responsibility.

Health Club - Comboni is a student-led initiative that promotes health awareness, personal hygiene, disease prevention and healthy habits. Through practical education and student participation, we aim to make health an important part of school life.

OUR VISION

A healthier community

To build a healthy, informed and responsible school community.

OUR MISSION

Learning through action

To make health education practical and engaging while encouraging good hygiene, teamwork and student leadership.

OUR 2026/2027 PLAN

Two terms. One purpose.

TERM ONE

Establishment & Awareness

  • Confirm school approval, leadership and membership.
  • Launch health tips, quizzes and the question box.
  • Begin short health lessons and monthly meetings.
  • Run Clean Hands, Bright Futures Hygiene Week.
  • Contact potential health and sanitation partners.
  • Review progress and finances.
TERM TWO

Activities & Recognition

  • Continue lessons, quizzes and monthly meetings.
  • Run the remaining themed Hygiene Weeks.
  • Seek training with qualified health workers, subject to approval.
  • Continue partnership requests and approved activities.
  • Review results and prepare an annual report.
  • Recognize active participants with certificates.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PURPOSE

Our leadership structure.

The club is guided by a supervising teacher and operates with school administration approval. Leadership roles may be adjusted according to available members.

President

Leads the club and coordinates its work.

Vice President

Supports the president and follows up on activities.

Secretary

Keeps meeting notes, records and announcements.

Finance Officer

Records membership contributions and expenses.

Health Education Coordinator

Helps prepare lessons, quizzes and health tips.

Hygiene Coordinator

Coordinates Hygiene Weeks and personal-hygiene awareness.

Media Officer

Manages approved communications and online engagement.

Partnerships & Activities Coordinator

Contacts potential partners and helps organize activities.

CELEBRATING CONTRIBUTION

Recognizing commitment.

At the end of the academic year, active participants may receive certificates recognizing meaningful involvement and contribution. Recognition is based on participation, not simply payment of a fee.
